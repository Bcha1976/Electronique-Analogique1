Linearising a diode around its operating point

The reference diode of the course (IS = 8.7 fA, n = 1, VT = 25.85 mV) fed by E0 through R, plus a small signal es. On the left, the characteristic, the load line and the tangent at Q; on the right, the exact current and the current predicted by the linear model.

Chapter 1, section 1.9

Things to observe
  • Course example: VQ ≈ 0.696 V, IQ ≈ 4.30 mA, rd ≈ 6.0 Ω, V0 ≈ 0.670 V.
  • Increase the amplitude of es: the two curves separate when δv approaches 2nV_T ≈ 52 mV.
  • “No bias”: E0 = 0 and Em = 0.72 V, the current becomes a train of pulses (figure 1.10, left).
