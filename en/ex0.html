What these exercises practise

This set accompanies chapter 0. It requires no calculation: it sets up the approach of the course (roles of the engineer, analysis and design, chain of reasoning) before the first circuits.

About these exercises

These exercises came at the end of chapter 0 of the course notes; they are gathered here. Their answers are folded under each statement (solutions book, same section).

Exercise C0.1Which role?

For each task, say whether it belongs mainly to the inventor, the researcher or the technician, and justify in one sentence: (a) troubleshooting a power supply that overheats; (b) establishing a model of the noise of a sensor; (c) proposing a device that charges a phone by walking.
