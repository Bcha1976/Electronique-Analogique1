INTERACTIVES

Interactives

Four interactives for the core of the course, each computed with the equations and reference values of the course notes, and also placed in its chapter, at the section it illustrates. The explorers, which are shorter, are in the chapters.

  1. Chapter 1Linearising a diode around its operating pointThe reference diode of the course (IS = 8.7 fA, n = 1, VT = 25.85 mV) fed by E0 through R, plus a small signal es.
  2. Chapter 2The RC circuit in time and in frequencyThe same RC circuit (R = 1 kΩ, C = 100 nF, τ = 100 µs) driven by a square wave: the time response (method 2) and the Bode plot (method 4), with the fundamental and the harmonics of the square wave placed on the gain curve..
  3. Chapter 3Four diode models, one circuitIdeal model, threshold model, threshold and resistance, exponential model: each model gives its point Q on the same load line.
  4. Chapter 4Generic circuit: from cut-off to saturationThe circuit of figure 4.7 (VCC = 10 V, RC = 1.25 kΩ, RB = 50 kΩ, β = 100, VA = 100 V), computed with the Ebers–Moll equations.
